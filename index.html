Project Overview

This project presents a structured web-based roadmap for documenting the methodological and statistical analysis process conducted for the course PHD-Course Research and Analytical Methods. The project is organized into two complementary analytical tracks: a full SPSS-based track and an integrated SPSS–AMOS track. Each stage includes a methodological description, downloadable analysis files, and a formatted preview of the corresponding Word report to facilitate supervisor review.

The purpose of this roadmap is to provide a transparent and organized presentation of the statistical analysis workflow, starting from data cleaning and preparation, moving through demographic analysis, descriptive statistics, assumption testing, reliability and validity assessment, correlation, regression, and mediation analysis, and ending with paper preparation and references. All analyses were conducted on a sample of N = 270 academic staff members at Al-Quds Open University (QOU), Palestine, examining the mediating role of Job Satisfaction in the relationship between Psychological Capital and Work Engagement.

Analytical Tracks

Track A: Full SPSS-Based Analysis

This track documents the complete analysis workflow using SPSS outputs, including data cleaning, descriptive and inferential statistics, reliability (Cronbach’s Alpha), exploratory factor analysis, correlation, regression, and PROCESS-based mediation procedures.

Track B: Integrated SPSS–AMOS Analysis

This track extends the SPSS workflow with AMOS-oriented structural modeling logic, supporting confirmatory factor analysis (CFA), model-based relationships, structural paths, and SEM-based mediation where applicable.
